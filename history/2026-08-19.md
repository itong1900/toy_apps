## Deals for August 19, 2026

The current market environment, characterized by a low VIX of 14.89, suggests a period of relative calm and potential investor complacency. In such conditions, option premiums are generally suppressed, making defined-risk strategies like credit spreads and iron condors particularly attractive. These strategies allow for income generation through time decay (theta) while strictly managing potential losses, which is prudent when volatility is low and can revert higher unexpectedly. The focus today is on high-probability setups on strong, liquid tickers that are either pulling back to support levels or trading within a predictable range.
