## Deals for September 21, 2026

Given the current market context with a VIX at 14.87, we are in a low volatility environment. This suggests a degree of market complacency and means that option premiums are relatively compressed. For option sellers, this requires careful strike selection to find an adequate risk/reward balance. The strategies outlined below will focus on high-probability, defined-risk setups that are well-suited for a stable to slightly bullish market, capitalizing on time decay (theta) while managing risk in this low-premium landscape.
