## Deals for October 14, 2026

As of 2026-10-14, the market exhibits a sense of calm, with the VIX at a relatively low 15.27. This environment generally translates to lower option premiums, making it a challenging landscape for premium sellers. However, it also suggests lower expected volatility, which can favor high-probability strategies like credit spreads and covered calls. The key is to be selective, focusing on strong technical levels in high-quality tickers and using defined-risk structures. The following conditional strategies are designed to trigger only at specific, advantageous price points, allowing traders to patiently wait for their optimal entry.

> **Ticker:** NVDA (NVIDIA Corp.)
