## Deals for August 22, 2026

Given the current market environment with a VIX at 15.13, implied volatility is relatively low, suggesting a degree of market complacency. This environment generally leads to lower option premiums, making it crucial to be selective when selling. The optimal approach is to focus on high-quality tickers, selling puts on technical pullbacks to support or selling calls into strength at resistance. This strategy aims for high-probability income generation while managing risk in a market that may not offer rich premiums for directionally neutral trades.
