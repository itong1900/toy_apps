## Deals for July 25, 2026

As of 2026-07-25, the market exhibits a tone of cautious optimism tempered by uncertainty. The VIX at 18.7 is not in panic territory but sits above its recent lows, indicating that option premiums are slightly elevated. This environment is favorable for premium-selling strategies, as the implied volatility priced into options may be higher than the volatility the market ultimately realizes. This provides a statistical edge to sellers. The focus today is on high-probability, defined-risk trades on quality large-cap tickers, capitalizing on expected price levels and this moderate volatility premium.

> **Reward / Risk:** Max Profit \$350 / Max Loss \$650 (Ratio: ~53%)

> **Ticker:** AAPL (Apple Inc.)
> **Strategy:** Sell Covered Call
> **Condition/Strike:** For shareholders holding at least 100 shares, if AAPL rallies to a resistance level near \$235, consider selling a \$240 or \$245 strike Call.
> **Expiration:** 14-30 DTE
> **Est. Premium:** \$1.50 - \$2.50 (for the \$240 strike)
> **Reward / Risk:** Max Profit = Premium Received + (Strike Price - Current Price). The primary risk is the opportunity cost if AAPL rallies significantly past the strike price.
> **Rationale:** This is a conservative income-generating strategy. It allows shareholders to monetize a position by selling out-of-the-money calls against their shares, generating cash flow while still participating in upside up to the strike price. It's effective for generating yield in a stable to slightly bullish market.
