This translates to reduced option premiums, making it a challenging environment for sellers seeking high yields. However, it is an excellent time to focus on high-probability, defined-risk strategies. We will focus on selling credit spreads and iron condors outside of expected trading ranges and employing covered calls on core holdings to generate steady cash flow.
