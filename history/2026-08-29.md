## Deals for August 29, 2026

The current market environment, with a VIX of 14.43, indicates low expected volatility and a degree of market complacency. In such conditions, option premiums are generally lower, making it crucial to select strategies with a high probability of success. Defined-risk trades like credit spreads and iron condors are particularly attractive as they manage risk effectively while still capitalizing on theta (time) decay. The focus will be on identifying strong technical levels in high-quality tickers and selling premium against them, anticipating a continuation of stable, range-bound, or slowly trending price action.
