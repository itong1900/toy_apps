## Deals for August 11, 2026

The current market environment, with a VIX at a relatively low 15.28 and major indices like SPY and QQQ at elevated levels, suggests a mood of cautious optimism or consolidation. This low implied volatility reduces the raw premium available for sellers, but it also points to market stability, which favors high-probability strategies. The ideal approach in this context is not to chase high premiums on risky bets, but to systematically generate income through defined-risk trades like credit spreads and iron condors on high-quality underlying assets. These strategies profit from time decay (theta) and are well-suited for a market that may trade sideways or grind slowly higher.
