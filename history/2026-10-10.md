## Deals for October 10, 2026

As of 2026-10-10, the market sentiment appears cautiously optimistic, characterized by a low VIX of 14.84. This environment suggests reduced fear and lower implied volatility, which translates to cheaper option premiums. While this makes selling premium for income less lucrative on an absolute basis, it favors high-probability, defined-risk strategies. The focus should be on selling spreads at key technical levels on high-quality underlying stocks, capturing theta decay while maintaining strict risk management.

> **Ticker:** MSFT (Microsoft Corp.)
> **Strategy:** Sell Put Spread (Bull Put)
> **Condition/Strike:** If MSFT pulls back to a support area around \$450, Sell the \$445 Put / Buy the \$435 Put.
> **Expiration:** 21-40 DTE (Days to Expiration)
> **Est. Premium:** \$2.50 - \$3.20
> **Reward / Risk:** Max Profit \$320 / Max Loss \$680 (Ratio: ~47%)
> **Rationale:** This strategy targets a high-probability entry at a significant technical support level for a blue-chip leader. Selling a put spread below this level offers a buffer and generates income with a clearly defined maximum loss, which is prudent in a low VIX environment.

> **Ticker:** AAPL (Apple Inc.)

> **Expiration:** 14-30 DTE
> **Est. Premium:** \$1.80 - \$2.50

> **Rationale:** A covered call is an excellent way to generate consistent income from an existing long-term holding. Selling calls into strength at a potential resistance area capitalizes on elevated short-term premiums and enhances the overall return of the position.
