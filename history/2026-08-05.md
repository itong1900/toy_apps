## Deals for August 05, 2026

The market today presents a picture of cautious optimism. With the VIX at a moderate 15.81, implied volatility is not excessively high, suggesting that while downside protection isn't cheap, option premiums are not overly inflated. This environment favors defined-risk strategies that profit from time decay and modest directional moves. We will focus on selling premium through high-probability setups like put credit spreads on fundamentally strong companies during minor pullbacks, covered calls to generate income on existing holdings, and iron condors on tickers that may enter a period of consolidation.
