## Deals for October 12, 2026

With the VIX at a low 14.84, the market is signaling complacency and reduced fear, which translates to lower option premiums. This environment is less than ideal for pure premium sellers but favors high-probability, defined-risk strategies. The focus should be on collecting consistent income through trades that profit from time decay and sideways or slowly trending price action. Spreads, such as put credit spreads and iron condors, are particularly attractive as they strictly define risk while maintaining a high probability of success. The following are conditional trade ideas designed to capitalize on this market mood.
