## Deals for August 14, 2026

The current market environment, with a VIX at a low 14.25, suggests a period of stability and lower expected volatility. This makes selling options for high premiums more challenging, demanding a focus on high-probability setups and tickers with relatively higher implied volatility. The strategy is to leverage well-defined technical levels and defined-risk structures like spreads. We will look to sell puts or put spreads below key support on strong uptrending names, and consider covered calls on positions that may be range-bound near resistance. For more volatile names like TSLA, an Iron Condor can capitalize on its higher intrinsic premium while defining risk on both sides.
