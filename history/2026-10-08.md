## Deals for October 08, 2026

With the VIX at a relatively low 15.08, the market is signaling a period of calm, which translates to lower overall option premiums. In this environment, selling naked options offers less compensation for the risk involved. Therefore, today's focus is on defined-risk strategies like credit spreads and iron condors. These setups offer a superior return on capital and a high probability of success by capitalizing on time decay (theta) while strictly managing potential losses. We will look for conditional setups on our target watchlist, entering trades as these high-quality tickers approach key technical levels of support or resistance.
